# Glossary: Phase 0

**Purpose**: Canonical definitions of business terms and acronyms used across all Phase 0 documentation.

## Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **AI Sales Engine** | The automated B2B outreach system that processes leads, generates personalized emails, and tracks engagement. |
| **B2B** | Business-to-Business. Refers to commerce or communication between businesses, not between a business and individual consumers. |
| **CTA** | Call-to-Action. The specific action requested in an outreach email (e.g., "Book a Site Visit"). |
| **Enrichment** | The process of augmenting extracted lead data with additional information (e.g., finding missing emails, LinkedIn profiles, company details). |
| **GCC** | Gulf Cooperation Council. The six member states: Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, and Oman. |
| **ICP** | Ideal Customer Profile. A set of criteria defining the target company segments, industries, geographies, and personas for outreach. |
| **KPI** | Key Performance Indicator. A measurable value that demonstrates how effectively objectives are being achieved. |
| **Lead** | A potential business contact extracted from a business card, PDF, or other source, who may become a customer. |
| **OCR** | Optical Character Recognition. Technology that converts images of text (e.g., business cards) into machine-readable text data. |
| **Outreach** | The process of initiating contact with potential leads via email or other communication channels. |
| **Persona** | A specific role or job title within a target company that the outreach is directed to (e.g., Facilities Manager). |
| **Pipeline** | The end-to-end flow of lead processing: Ingestion → Extraction → Enrichment → Generation → Delivery → Tracking. |
| **Reply Rate** | The percentage of outreach emails that receive a response from the recipient. |
| **Scoring** | A numerical evaluation of a lead's quality based on criteria such as industry relevance, website presence, and email validity. |
| **Site Visit** | An in-person consultation or meeting at the lead's location; the primary CTA for TRIPLE MS outreach. |
| **TRIPLE MS** | The company offering large-scale diesel generators and power solutions in the GCC region. |
| **Vision AI** | AI-powered image analysis capable of extracting structured data from unstructured visual inputs (e.g., business cards). |
