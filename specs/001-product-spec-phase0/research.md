# Research: Product Specification Phase 0

## Market Context: GCC Industrial & Construction Sectors (2025-2026)
Research indicates a massive growth trajectory for the GCC diesel generator market, driven largely by infrastructure projects (Vision 2030 in KSA), AI workloads (Data Centers requiring Tier-3 uptime), and general manufacturing growth. The market is projected to grow significantly.

**Key Segments for TRIPLE MS:**
1. **Construction & Infrastructure**: Massive ongoing mega-projects (e.g., NEOM in KSA, UAE expansions) requiring temporary and prime power before grid connectivity.
2. **Data Centers & Technology**: Rapid expansion of AI data centers requiring reliable Tier-3 backup power.
3. **Industrial Manufacturing & Logistics**: Continuous operations demanding uninterrupted power to prevent costly downtime.

## Target Persona: Facilities & Operations Managers
**Roles & Responsibilities:**
- Ensuring operational continuity and uptime.
- Managing equipment lifecycle and maintenance budgets.
- Mitigating risks associated with power outages.

**Key Pain Points (2025 Context):**
- **Downtime Costs**: Any power interruption translates to massive financial losses, especially in manufacturing and data centers.
- **Predictive Maintenance**: Shifting from reactive repairs to predictive maintenance to avoid catastrophic failures.

## Decision: Documentation Formats and Standards
The deliverable for Phase 0 is a formal Product Specification including ICP, Use Cases, and Success Metrics. We will use Markdown as the primary format to ensure compatibility with the Spec Kit and version control.

- **ICP Structure**: Will follow the industrial/GCC focus identified in clarifications.
- **Use Case Modeling**: Will use Gherkin-style (Given/When/Then) scenarios for testable functional flows.
- **Success Metrics**: Will be categorized by pipeline stage (Extraction, Generation, Delivery) to align with project KPIs.

## Rationale
- **Markdown**: Industry standard for technical documentation, easily searchable, and integrates with the current project structure.
- **Gherkin**: Provides unambiguous, testable requirements that bridge the gap between business needs and technical implementation.
- **Pipeline-based Metrics**: Ensures that every architectural component has a corresponding performance target.

## Alternatives Considered
- **PDF/DocX**: Rejected due to poor version control integration and difficulty in automated parsing.
- **UML Diagrams**: Deferred to Phase 1 (System Design) as Phase 0 focuses on business logic rather than technical architecture.
